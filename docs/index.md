# Privacy Policy — Toris Collection

**Last updated: November 7, 2026**

This is the privacy policy for the **Toris Collection** Android app
(package `com.toriscollection.app`, [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.toriscollection.app)).
Toris Collection is an independent project. Contact: [rokkyofarm@gmail.com](mailto:rokkyofarm@gmail.com)

## The short version

- **There is no account and no sign-in.** You never give us your name, email or phone number to use the app.
- **Your garden is saved only on your own phone.** It is not sent to us or stored on any server.
- **The app shows ads through Google AdMob.** AdMob may use your device's advertising ID. This is the only data that leaves your phone.
- **We do not sell your data.**

## What the app collects, and why

### 1. Advertising data (Google AdMob)

The app shows a small banner ad and an optional reward ad (you choose whether to watch it;
skipping it changes nothing in your garden, field guide or radio). These ads are provided by
**Google AdMob**. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and use:

- your device's **advertising ID**
- approximate location derived from your IP address
- device and app information (for example device model, OS version, app version)
- ad interactions (for example whether an ad was shown or tapped) and diagnostics

This data is collected and processed by Google under
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
[how Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
We do not receive your advertising ID or any personal information from AdMob.

### 2. Your garden (stays on your phone)

Your progress — the land you chose, what you planted, the birds in your field guide, alarm
settings and language — is saved **only in the app's storage on your phone**. It is not sent to us.

The "Move your garden" screen can show a **transfer code** so you can move your garden to a
new phone. The code is created on your phone and only goes where you copy it yourself.

## What the app does not do

- It does not require an account or a login.
- It does not collect your name, email, phone number or contacts.
- It does not access your precise location, microphone, camera, photos or files.
- It does not include analytics or tracking SDKs other than Google AdMob.

## Permissions the app uses

- **Internet / network state** — to load ads.
- **Notifications, alarms, foreground media playback, wake lock** — to play the bird-song alarm and
  the garden radio, including when the screen is off.

## Your choices

- **Advertising ID:** you can reset it or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings
  (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
- **Your garden:** uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it from your phone.
- Questions or requests: [rokkyofarm@gmail.com](mailto:rokkyofarm@gmail.com)

## Children

Toris Collection is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal
information from children.

## Open data used in the app

Bird recordings come from [xeno-canto](https://xeno-canto.org) under their Creative Commons
licenses, with each recordist credited in the app. Feeding relationships come from
[GloBI (Global Biotic Interactions)](https://www.globalbioticinteractions.org).
These are sources of bird and ecology data, not data about you.

## Changes

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top.
